The equations of motion for the cart and the pole are as follows, where:


g = gravity = 9.8 m/s^2


J = inertial constant of the pole


x = cart position


th = theta = pole angle w.r.t. vertical up, +90deg when horizontal to the right


m = mass of the pole


m\_c = mass of the cart


M = m + m\_c


 $\mu_c$ = friction coefficient of the cart


r = radius of the cart wheels


f = force applied to the cart (via the torque generated by its motors)


l = location of the CoM of the pole


O = origin


Q = CoM of the pole


P = pivot point of the pole


C = CoM of the Cart

```matlab
syms m_c m x_ddot x_dot x theta_ddot theta_dot theta l mu_c J g f r
% eqtn 12-F
M= m_c + m;
Ff = -mu_c*M*g*x_dot/r; % look more closely at friction and how it applies force to the cart's movement
eqn_cart = m_c*x_ddot + m*(l*theta_ddot*cos(theta) - l*theta_dot^2*sin(theta) + x_ddot) - Ff == f
```
eqn_cart = 
 $\displaystyle m\,{\left(-l\,\sin \left(\theta \right)\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 +\ddot{x} +l\,\ddot{\theta} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)\right)}+m_c \,\ddot{x} +\frac{g\,\mu_c \,\dot{x} \,{\left(m+m_c \right)}}{r}=f$
 

```matlab
% eqtn 18
eqn_pend = m*l*(l*theta_ddot + x_ddot*cos(theta)) - m*g*l*sin(theta) + J*theta_ddot == 0
```
eqn_pend = 
 $\displaystyle J\,\ddot{\theta} +l\,m\,{\left(l\,\ddot{\theta} +\ddot{x} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)\right)}-g\,l\,m\,\sin \left(\theta \right)=0$
 

```matlab

% Deriving the solutions for ONLY x_ddot and theta_ddot
[x_ddot_sol, theta_ddot_sol] = solve([eqn_pend,eqn_cart],[x_ddot,theta_ddot])
```
x_ddot_sol = 
 $\displaystyle -\frac{g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} -f\,l^2 \,m\,r-l^3 \,m^2 \,r\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 \,\sin \left(\theta \right)-J\,f\,r+J\,g\,m\,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +J\,g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +g\,l^2 \,m\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)\,\sin \left(\theta \right)-J\,l\,m\,r\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 \,\sin \left(\theta \right)}{r\,{\left(-l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\theta \right)}^2 +l^2 \,m^2 +m_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,m+J\,m_c \right)}}$
 
theta_ddot_sol = 
 $\displaystyle \frac{l\,m\,{\left(-l\,m\,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)\,\sin \left(\theta \right)\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 -f\,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)+g\,m\,r\,\sin \left(\theta \right)+g\,m_c \,r\,\sin \left(\theta \right)+g\,m\,\mu_c \,\dot{x} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)+g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)\right)}}{r\,{\left(-l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\theta \right)}^2 +l^2 \,m^2 +m_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,m+J\,m_c \right)}}$
 

# Setting up the State\-space equations
```matlab
syms beta_1 beta_2 beta_3 beta_4 u t
% f = [beta_1_dot; beta_2_dot; beta_3_dot; beta_4_dot] 
%   = [x_dot; x_ddot; theta_dot; theta_ddot]
ss_x_theta = [x_dot;
    x_ddot_sol;
    theta_dot;
    theta_ddot_sol]
```
ss_x_theta = 
 $\displaystyle \begin{array}{l} \left(\begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline -\frac{g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} -f\,l^2 \,m\,r-l^3 \,m^2 \,r\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 \,\sin \left(\theta \right)-J\,f\,r+J\,g\,m\,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +J\,g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +g\,l^2 \,m\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} +g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)\,\sin \left(\theta \right)-J\,l\,m\,r\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 \,\sin \left(\theta \right)}{\sigma_1 }\newline \dot{\theta} \newline \frac{l\,m\,{\left(-l\,m\,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)\,\sin \left(\theta \right)\,{\dot{\theta} }^2 -f\,r\,\cos \left(\theta \right)+g\,m\,r\,\sin \left(\theta \right)+g\,m_c \,r\,\sin \left(\theta \right)+g\,m\,\mu_c \,\dot{x} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)+g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\dot{x} \,\cos \left(\theta \right)\right)}}{\sigma_1 } \end{array}\right)\\\mathrm{}\\\textrm{where}\\\mathrm{}\\\;\;\sigma_1 =r\,{\left(-l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\theta \right)}^2 +l^2 \,m^2 +m_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,m+J\,m_c \right)}\end{array}$
 

```matlab
ss_beta = subs(ss_x_theta,[x, x_dot, theta, theta_dot, f],[beta_1, beta_2, beta_3, beta_4, u]);
beta = [beta_1; beta_2; beta_3; beta_4];
% Linearize the system
% per Lecture 2 of ME6401 Linear Controls, take the Jacobian of the system
% of equations to get the partial differential wrt each state variable, and
% the input variable
Asym = jacobian(ss_beta,beta)
```
Asym = 
 $\displaystyle \begin{array}{l} \left(\begin{array}{cccc} 0 & 1 & 0 & 0\newline 0 & -\frac{g\,\mu_c \,l^2 \,m^2 +g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,g\,\mu_c \,m+J\,g\,m_c \,\mu_c }{r\,\sigma_1 } & \frac{r\,{\beta_4 }^2 \,l^3 \,m^2 \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)+J\,r\,{\beta_4 }^2 \,l\,m\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)-g\,r\,l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 +g\,r\,l^2 \,m^2 \,{\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 }{r\,\sigma_1 }+\frac{2\,l^2 \,m^2 \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\,{\left(\beta_2 \,g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,\mu_c -l^2 \,m\,r\,u-{\beta_4 }^2 \,l^3 \,m^2 \,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)-J\,r\,u+J\,\beta_2 \,g\,m\,\mu_c +J\,\beta_2 \,g\,m_c \,\mu_c +\beta_2 \,g\,l^2 \,m\,m_c \,\mu_c +g\,l^2 \,m^2 \,r\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)-J\,{\beta_4 }^2 \,l\,m\,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\right)}}{r\,{\sigma_1 }^2 } & \frac{2\,\beta_4 \,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\,l^3 \,m^2 +2\,J\,\beta_4 \,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\,l\,m}{r\,\sigma_1 }\newline 0 & 0 & 0 & 1\newline 0 & \frac{l\,m\,{\left(g\,m\,\mu_c \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)+g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\right)}}{r\,\sigma_1 } & \frac{l\,m\,{\left(r\,u\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)+g\,m\,r\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)+g\,m_c \,r\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)-{\beta_4 }^2 \,l\,m\,r\,{\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 +{\beta_4 }^2 \,l\,m\,r\,{\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 -\beta_2 \,g\,m\,\mu_c \,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)-\beta_2 \,g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\right)}}{r\,\sigma_1 }-\frac{2\,l^3 \,m^3 \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\,{\left(-l\,m\,r\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)\,{\beta_4 }^2 -r\,u\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)+g\,m\,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)+g\,m_c \,r\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)+\beta_2 \,g\,m\,\mu_c \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)+\beta_2 \,g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\right)}}{r\,{\sigma_1 }^2 } & -\frac{2\,\beta_4 \,l^2 \,m^2 \,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)\,\sin \left(\beta_3 \right)}{\sigma_1 } \end{array}\right)\\\mathrm{}\\\textrm{where}\\\mathrm{}\\\;\;\sigma_1 =-l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 +l^2 \,m^2 +m_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,m+J\,m_c \end{array}$
 

```matlab
Bsym = jacobian(ss_beta,u)
```
Bsym = 
 $\displaystyle \begin{array}{l} \left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline \frac{m\,r\,l^2 +J\,r}{r\,\sigma_1 }\newline 0\newline -\frac{l\,m\,\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)}{\sigma_1 } \end{array}\right)\\\mathrm{}\\\textrm{where}\\\mathrm{}\\\;\;\sigma_1 =-l^2 \,m^2 \,{\cos \left(\beta_3 \right)}^2 +l^2 \,m^2 +m_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,m+J\,m_c \end{array}$
 

```matlab

% per problem diagram, setting equilibrium point at theta = 0 (vertical) and
% arbitrarily selecting x = 0 for simplicity
A0 = subs(Asym,[beta_1, beta_2, beta_3, beta_4], [0, 0, 0, 0])
```
A0 = 
 $\displaystyle \begin{array}{l} \left(\begin{array}{cccc} 0 & 1 & 0 & 0\newline 0 & -\frac{g\,\mu_c \,l^2 \,m^2 +g\,m_c \,\mu_c \,l^2 \,m+J\,g\,\mu_c \,m+J\,g\,m_c \,\mu_c }{r\,\sigma_1 } & -\frac{g\,l^2 \,m^2 }{\sigma_1 } & 0\newline 0 & 0 & 0 & 1\newline 0 & \frac{l\,m\,{\left(g\,m\,\mu_c +g\,m_c \,\mu_c \right)}}{r\,\sigma_1 } & \frac{l\,m\,{\left(g\,m\,r+g\,m_c \,r\right)}}{r\,\sigma_1 } & 0 \end{array}\right)\\\mathrm{}\\\textrm{where}\\\mathrm{}\\\;\;\sigma_1 =m\,m_c \,l^2 +J\,m+J\,m_c \end{array}$
 

```matlab
B0 = subs(Bsym,[beta_1, beta_2, beta_3, beta_4], [0, 0, 0, 0])
```
B0 = 
 $\displaystyle \left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline \frac{m\,r\,l^2 +J\,r}{r\,{\left(m\,m_c \,l^2 +J\,m+J\,m_c \right)}}\newline 0\newline -\frac{l\,m}{m\,m_c \,l^2 +J\,m+J\,m_c } \end{array}\right)$
 

```matlab

% Thus, our linearized state-space representation of the system can be
% represented as: diff(beta,t) = A0*beta + B0*u

% where beta_3 is the deviation from the equil point, theta = 0
```

# Inserting real values and creating the State Space object
```matlab
g_real = 9.8; % [m/sec^2]
m_motor = 9.5*2/1000; % [kg] documentation
m_bump = 35/1000; % [kg] documentation
m_batt = 138/1000; % [kg] measured
m_wheels = 39.7/1000; % [kg] documentation
m_cb = (200/1000 - m_motor - m_bump - m_wheels); % [kg] measured value
m_c_real = (m_wheels); % [kg] - mass of cart (i.e. the wheels of the segway)
m_real = m_motor + m_bump + m_batt + m_cb; % [kg] - mass of "pole"
mu_c_real = 0.02; % bearing viscous friction coeff. for rotational speed

% Calculating CoM of the pole w.r.t. location of axis of rotation
x_rot = 31/1000; % [m] axis of rotation is NOT at the bottom of the pole
x_motor = 0/1000; % [m] motor w.r.t. rotation axis
l_cb = 109.2/1000; % [m] lengh of control board
x_cb = l_cb/2 - x_rot; % [m] control board w.r.t. rotation axis
l_batt = 95.7/1000; % [m] length of batteries
x_batt = l_batt/2 - x_rot + 2.5/1000; % [m] batteries w.r.t. rotation axis
x_bump = l_cb - 30/1000 + 40/1000 - x_rot; % [m] bumpers w.r.t. rotation axis
y_motor = 16.416/1000; y_cb = 0; y_batt = 0; y_bump = 0; % [m] w.r.t. rotation axis
CM_x_rot = (m_motor*x_motor + m_cb*x_cb + m_batt*x_batt + m_bump*x_bump)/(m_real); % [m]
CM_y_rot = y_motor*m_motor/m_real; % [m]
CM_rot = sqrt(CM_x_rot^2 + CM_y_rot^2); % [m] length from CM of pole to axis of rotation

l_real = CM_rot; % [m] - length to pole CoM

% Calculating Inertia of the Pole:
I_motor = m_motor*y_motor^2; % [kg m^2] point mass inertia of motors
I_bump = m_bump*x_bump^2; % [kg m^2] point mass inertia of bumpers
% Parallel axis theorem: I = Icm + m h^2
% Inertia of a pole of equally distributed mass about one end: I = m l^2/3 
% Inertia of a pole of equally distributed mass about the CoM: I = m l^2/12
I_batt = m_batt*(l_batt/2)^2/12 + m_batt*x_batt^2; % [kg m^2] inertia of batteries
I_cb = m_cb*(l_cb/2)^2/12 + m_cb*x_cb^2; % [kg m^2] inertia of control board

J_real = (I_motor + I_bump + I_batt + I_cb); % kg*m^2 - mass moment of inertia of the pole (1/12*m*(length)^2)

r_real = 40/1000; % [m]

symvar(A0)
```
ans = 
 $\displaystyle \left(\begin{array}{ccccccc} J & g & l & m & m_c  & \mu_c  & r \end{array}\right)$

```matlab
symvar(B0)
```

 $\displaystyle \left(\begin{array}{ccccc} J & l & m & m_c  & r \end{array}\right)$

# Stabilize the System with SS Feedback PI control

With integral control, we have to define u as follows, introducing an additional state to the system since we are increasing the order of the transfer function:


 $\dot{x} =Ax+Bu$  , $y=Cx$ 

 $ u=-K_p x-K_i x_i $ 

 $ x_i =\int_0^t e~d\tau ~\,~where~e=-y_d +x $ 

yd is the desired output of the x position of the cart.


NOTE: not using integral control on the angle of the pendulum since it led to unstable control. Likely the two integrating errors were fighting each other causing an undesireable control scenario (to be looked into in the future).


Thus we have:

 $ $ \left(\begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline \dot{x_i }  \end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}{cc} (A-BK_p ) & -BK_i \newline \begin{array}{cccc} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} & 0
\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)+\left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline -y_d  \end{array}\right) $ $ 

In another representation (more intuitive for using the Bass\-Gura solution for placing the poles via the acker() or place() command):

 $ \left(\begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline \dot{x_i }  \end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}{cc} A & 0\newline C(1,:) & 0 \end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)-\left(\begin{array}{c} B\newline 0 \end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{cc} K_p  & K_i  \end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)+\left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline -y_d  \end{array}\right) $ 

 $ \Rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline \dot{x_i }  \end{array}\right)=\bar{A} \left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)-\bar{B} K\left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)+\left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline -y_d  \end{array}\right) $ 

 $\Rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline \dot{x_i }  \end{array}\right)=\hat{A} \left(\begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i  \end{array}\right)+\left(\begin{array}{c} 0\newline -y_d  \end{array}\right)$ , where $\hat{A} =\bar{A} -\bar{B} K$

# Stabilize System with Luenberger Observer Feedback & PI Control
## Theory:

With integral control, we have to define u as follows, introducing an additional state to the system since we are increasing the order of the transfer function:


Plant: $\dot{x} =Ax+Bu$  , $y=Cx$ 


Observer: $\dot{\hat{x} } =A\hat{x} +Bu+L(y-C\hat{x} )$ 


Controller: 

 $ u=-K_s \hat{x} -K_i x_i $ 

 $ x_i =\int_0^t e~d\tau ~\,~where~e=y_d -C_2 \hat{y} $ 

Diagram:


![image_2.png](wheeled_balancer_model_media/image_2.png)


NOTE: since we only care about controlling for the x\-position of the cart, the $y_d$ in the diagram above is only $x_d (1)$ and $C_2 \hat{y} =C_2 C\hat{x} =\left\lbrack \begin{array}{cccc} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \hat{x}$ 


Controller Subsystem:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \dot{x} =Ax+B(-K_s \hat{x} -K_i x_i ) $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; note: $\tilde{x} =x-\hat{x} \Rightarrow \hat{x} =x-\tilde{x}$ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \Rightarrow \dot{x} =Ax+B(-K_s (x-\tilde{x} )-K_i x_i )=(A-BK_s )x+BK_s \tilde{x} -BK_i x_i $ 

Observer Subsystem:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \dot{\hat{x} } =A\hat{x} +Bu+L(y-C\hat{x} ) $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; note: 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \dot{\tilde{x} } =\dot{x} -\dot{\hat{x} } =Ax+Bu-A\hat{x} -Bu-L(y-C\hat{x} )=A\tilde{x} -L(Cx-C\hat{x} ) $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \Rightarrow \dot{\tilde{x} } =(A-LC)\tilde{x} $ 

Integral Subsystem:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ {\dot{x} }_i =e=y_d -C_2 \hat{y} =y_d -C_2 C\hat{x} =y_d -C^{\prime } x+C^{\prime } \tilde{x} $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; where $C_2 C=C^{\prime }$ 


Overall Closed Loop System:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ \left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} \dot{x} \newline {\dot{x} }_i \newline \dot{\tilde{x} }  \end{array}\right\rbrack =\left\lbrack \begin{array}{ccc} A-BK_s  & -BK_i  & BK_s \newline -C^{\prime }  & 0 & C^{\prime } \newline 0 & 0 & A-LC \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i \newline \tilde{x}  \end{array}\right\rbrack +\left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} 0\newline y_d \newline 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $ =\left\lbrack \begin{array}{ccc} A & 0 & 0\newline -C^{\prime }  & 0 & C^{\prime } \newline 0 & 0 & A-LC \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i \newline \tilde{x}  \end{array}\right\rbrack -\left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} B\newline 0\newline 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack \begin{array}{ccc} K_s  & K_i  & -K_s  \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} x\newline x_i \newline \tilde{x}  \end{array}\right\rbrack +\left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} 0\newline y_d \newline 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack $ 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; where we let:


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $\bar{A} =\left\lbrack \begin{array}{cc} A & 0\newline -C^{\prime }  & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ , $\bar{B} =\left\lbrack \begin{array}{c} B\newline 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ , $K=\left\lbrack \begin{array}{cc} K_s  & K_i  \end{array}\right\rbrack$ 


Separation Principle:

-  The eigenvalues of the overall closed loop system are the **union** of the eigenvalues of the two subsystems:  
-  1. $\bar{A} -\bar{B} K$ (i.e. closed loop poles) 
-  2. A \- LC (i.e. observer poles) 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; By designing the location of the closed loop poles and the observer poles independently, we can still achieve a stable system with the desired performance.


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; e.g. K = acker(Abar,Bbar,pd\_control) and L = place(A',C',pd\_observer)
